TERMS OF USE / DRAFT

Use data responsibly.

These launch-stage terms set the intended rules for using the JP Signals website and API. They will be finalized before paid plans become available.

Service scope

JP Signals organizes selected public Japanese corporate and procurement records into searchable API responses. It is an information product, not legal, financial, investment, procurement, credit, employment, or insurance advice.

Source verification

Each supported record retains a source URL. Customers are responsible for verifying material facts against the official source before acting on them. Public records can be delayed, incomplete, corrected, or unavailable.

Permitted use

Use the API only through an authorized key or marketplace subscription, within the selected plan's quota and applicable source terms. Do not bypass authentication, rate limits, access controls, or use the service to build high-risk decisions about individuals.

Availability and changes

The service may change, pause, or remove an endpoint when necessary for security, data-source compliance, or reliability. Planned plan limits and update intervals are described on the data-sources page and in API documentation.

Payments

Paid subscriptions will be billed by the selected marketplace or payment provider under its billing terms. A paid plan does not transfer ownership of the underlying public records or source databases.

This is a launch-stage product notice, not legal advice. Obtain a jurisdiction-appropriate review before accepting paid subscriptions.
