DATA POLICY / TRANSPARENCY

Trace every signal.

JP Signals is designed so that a customer can trace each returned record back to an official source and see when it was collected.

Current source

The current dataset is derived from gBizINFO public corporate information and procurement-related records. Use is subject to the applicable gBizINFO terms. Every record served by the API retains source name, source URL, source license summary, and collection time.

Coverage boundary

The launch dataset currently emphasizes company profiles and procurement signals. It is not a complete registry of every Japanese business event. Some official endpoints may be unavailable or excluded when they do not support a compliant, reliable import path.

Freshness

Collection time is returned with the record and source summaries are available from the authenticated /v1/sources endpoint. Scheduled refreshes and backup checks must be operating before paid plans are enabled; until then, the displayed collection time is the authoritative freshness indicator.

Quality and corrections

We preserve source links, validate basic record structure, and avoid inventing missing values. If an official source corrects or removes a record, a later refresh may change or remove the corresponding API result.

For material decisions, verify the official source linked in the record. Do not treat the activity score as a prediction or a credit, employment, insurance, or eligibility decision.
