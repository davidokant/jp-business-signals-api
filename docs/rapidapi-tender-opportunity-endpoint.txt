Name: Search Japan Government Tender Opportunities

Description:
Search official Japanese public tender opportunities before award. Results include the Japanese tender title, buyer, geography, category, procedure, qualification, available dates, official source URL, source attribution, and retrieval time. Announcement text and attachments are deliberately excluded.

Path:
/v1/tenders/search

Method:
GET

Query parameters:
q: STRING, required. Tender search terms. Example: cloud.
buyer: STRING, optional. Buyer or agency name.
prefecture: STRING, optional. English or Japanese prefecture name, or two-digit JIS code. Example: Tokyo.
category: STRING, optional. One of goods, construction, services.
published_from: DATE, optional. ISO date, inclusive.
published_to: DATE, optional. ISO date, inclusive.
limit: NUMBER, optional. Default 25, maximum 100.
offset: NUMBER, optional. Default 0, maximum 900.
